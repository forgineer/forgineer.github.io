Start a conversation

Follow these steps to connect and chat with Ollama.

1
Create/Verify your Ollama account
Ensure you have access to models on ollama.com.
2
Enter your API Key and Config
Click the gear icon to enter your API key. It's stored entirely in your local browser storage.
3
Select a Model to chat
Once models fetch successfully, choose a model from the header dropdown menu to start typing.
Note: Connecting directly to remote hosts from a web browser is subject to cross-origin security rules (CORS). For local instances, set `OLLAMA_ORIGINS="*"` when starting your Ollama server.
Settings

These settings are stored completely locally in your web browser.

Default: https://ollama.com. Change this to http://localhost:11434 to connect to a local instance.
Your key is used to authorize requests with the configured host and is saved in your local storage.
About TryOllama

A quick, private way to chat with Ollama models from your browser.

Purpose

TryOllama is designed to be a quick and easy way to use Ollama in the cloud or locally with a focus on simplicity and privacy.

Data Storage

All preferences and settings, including your API key and selected model, are stored locally in your browser.

Privacy

No conversations are stored by the app or in your browser.

Portability

You can export your conversations or upload them for later use, but this is completely opt-in. No data is saved unless you choose to download it.

This application is hosted statically on GitHub and connects directly to your endpoints.
